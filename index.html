About the model ▾

Freight moves over the ORBIS network (Scheidel & Meeks, Stanford): 678 sites, 2,208 road, river and sea segments, each with a real cost in denarii per kg of grain — the same unit as Diocletian's Price Edict, so a good's price in any city is its origin price plus real freight along the cheapest route.

Cities eat their province's rural surplus first, then buy grain from the exporting provinces — Egypt, Africa, Sicily — cheapest delivered price first. Sea freight is ~50× cheaper than road, which is why everything important happens on the water.

Populations are scholarly estimates for the Severan peak (total ≈ 56 M). Consumption rates and mine outputs are stylised; prices and freight are the real data.
